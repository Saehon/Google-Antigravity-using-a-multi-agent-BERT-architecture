### Governance
- Provider and model adapters remain replaceable technology components.
- Model confidence or agent consensus is not treated as professional evidence.
- Synthetic/digital-twin outcomes must be labelled separately from observed data.
- Human review remains required for material accounting/audit conclusions.
- External provider names identify public technologies, methodological inspiration, or interoperability targets only unless a documented relationship exists.
