# Multi-Agent Accounting AI Framework Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Stable research baseline
- preserve reproducible execution for existing BERT/ML/agent experiments;
- classify legacy files and generated artifacts;
- document data provenance and third-party rights;
- maintain a vendor-neutral digital-twin architecture.

## Phase 2 — Agent interoperability
- formalize agent cards, tool contracts, and evidence interfaces;
- evaluate A2A/MCP-style interoperability where appropriate;
- separate model-provider adapters from accounting/audit logic;
- add deterministic validators for material calculations and controls.

## Phase 3 — Scientific-discovery workflow
- competing-hypothesis generation and critique;
- reproducible empirical conversion;
- evolutionary comparison of measures/models/specifications;
- latent-structure analysis with economic/accounting interpretation;
- adversarial critic, defender, replicator, and falsifier roles.

## Phase 4 — Benchmarking
- frozen train/validation/holdout boundaries;
- temporal and cross-domain testing;
- calibration, robustness and abstention evaluation;
- evidence-provenance and contradiction metrics;
- publish failures and limitations alongside successful runs.

## Phase 5 — NAAIL integration
- integrate selected modules with AAA, IFRS-AI Inspector, KIWI, ICFR research, and POMELO/VERA interfaces;
- preserve Human Gate requirements;
- keep private/patent-sensitive implementation separate from permissively licensed public research code.

## Boundary

This roadmap describes research directions only. It does not imply affiliation with Google, Microsoft, OpenAI, audit firms, regulators, or standard setters, and it does not establish production readiness or regulatory compliance.
