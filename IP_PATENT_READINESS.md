## Core rule

A GitHub commit can provide a dated technical record, but it **does not create patent rights, reserve patentability, or substitute for filing a patent application**. Public disclosure can affect novelty and patentability depending on jurisdiction and timing.

Potentially patentable original implementation details should remain private until explicit IP review and a release decision are complete.

## Pre-publication invention gate

Before publishing a novel multi-agent workflow, accounting-risk model, explainability mechanism, audit orchestration method, evaluation framework, synthetic-data method, or related technical contribution:

1. record inventors, conception date, technical problem, technical solution, alternatives, experiments, diagrams, and source-commit hashes;
2. search prior art and relevant scientific/patent literature;
3. separate third-party/open-source components from original contribution;
4. mark the invention **PRIVATE — PATENT REVIEW PENDING**;
5. obtain qualified patent/legal review where protection is contemplated;
6. choose patent filing first, private/trade-secret treatment, defensive publication, or public research release;
7. disclose enabling technical details only after that decision.

## Licensing boundary

Existing MIT/Apache rights already granted for covered material remain controlling. This file does not revoke those rights. Future patent-sensitive or non-commercial NAAIL modules should be clearly separated before public release.

## Research integrity

Patent strategy is distinct from scientific validity. Research claims remain subject to literature validation, reproducibility, falsification, adversarial review, provenance, and human approval.
